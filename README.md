# Yale Youth Poll — Crosstab Explorer

Browse weighted crosstabs from Yale Youth Poll surveys. Everything runs in your
browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

**Two weights, and they are not interchangeable.** `National` covers all registered
voters. `Youth` covers registered voters aged 18–34 and is the one to use for any
figure about young people — the 18–34 rows of a nationally weighted table are not the
youth estimate, because national weighting only controls composition at the national
margin.

Subgroups with fewer than 100 respondents are suppressed and shown as an em dash.

Weighted to the Catalist voter file by age interacted with gender, race, and education.
Full methodology accompanies each wave's published results at
[youthpoll.yale.edu](https://youthpoll.yale.edu).

Respondent-level data for Spring 2026 is also available from Yale's data repository.
